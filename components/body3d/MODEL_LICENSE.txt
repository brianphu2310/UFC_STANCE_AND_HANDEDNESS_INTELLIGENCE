fighter.glb is generated by scripts/build_body_model.py from MakeHuman assets:
the hm08 base mesh, the default skeleton and skin weights, and the macrodetails
targets (https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman).

Those assets were released by the MakeHuman team as CC0 1.0 (public domain
dedication) in September 2020. Copyright holders at the time of release:
Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier. No attribution is required;
it is given here as a courtesy.

The fist pose, the clothing shells (shorts, waistband, gloves, straps, ankle
bands), the reduced skeleton and the "heavy"/"lean" morph targets were made by
the build script.
